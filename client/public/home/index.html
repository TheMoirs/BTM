How a season runs in PTM

PTM is a simple round-robin tournament manager: every team in a division plays every other, followed by knockout finals if you want them.

Step 1 · Set up

Your league, your rules

Name the tournament, choose the number of divisions and games per match, add pistes and knockout stages, and set your own points for wins, losses and no-shows.

  • Registration closing date and entry fee
  • Quarter-finals, semi-finals and finals
  • Attach your rules as a PDF

Step 2 · Registration

Teams sign up, and pay, themselves

Send captains your registration link. They enter their team, contact details and players, then pay the entry fee straight away by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through a secure Stripe checkout. The money goes straight to you.

  • Confirmation email with a link to change details
  • Paid column shows who has paid
  • Tap a captain's phone or email to message them

Already have a list?

Import your teams from Excel

Download the sample file, paste in your teams and divisions, and upload it. Your whole league is set up in seconds.

Download the sample file (Excel)

Step 3 · Fixtures and scores

Generate every match in one click

Press Generate Matches, and PTM draws up the full round robin for each division. Find any match by team name, add the date and type in the game scores. Or, simply copy & paste the result from WhatsApp or Email. Tick NS for a no-show.

Paste result

Copy and paste results from WhatsApp or email

Simply copy a result message from WhatsApp or email and paste it in. PTM reads the teams and game scores, and the leaderboard is updated immediately.

Step 4 · Results

Tables that update themselves

The leaderboard, forecast and detailed results stay up to date for everyone. Players tap their team name for a stats card with form, win rate, best win and closest game, then share it to the team chat as an image.

Score Flip

Free for everyone

Toss, play and keep score on the piste

Score Flip is a phone app for the game itself. Flip the coin to see who throws the jack, pick your teams, and keep score with big up and down buttons and a match timer. When the match ends, share the result straight to your group chat.

You don't need PTM to use it. Score Flip is free for any game of pétanque, with no account.
